Save your work first

Close your applications. Keep the Beamo USB and the drive you intend to erase connected.

This version restarts before erasing any drive. After startup, you choose the disk and confirm the erase. You can keep working by closing this window.

Need help starting from the USB?

Keep the USB connected. Restart, then press the boot-menu key for your PC and choose the USB.

Dell, Lenovo, Acer
F12
HP
Esc, then F9
ASUS
F8 or Esc
MSI, Gigabyte
F11 (MSI), F12 (Gigabyte)

Windows 11 also offers Settings → System → Recovery → Advanced startup. On Windows 10, use Settings → Update & Security → Recovery. Choose Restart now, then Use a device.

If the USB is missing, try a direct USB port instead of a hub. Firmware settings and Secure Boot trust may affect whether it starts. See START-HERE.html on the USB for more help.

Not Apple Silicon. Not Chromebooks. Intel Mac booting is best-effort and this desktop launcher does not run on macOS.
