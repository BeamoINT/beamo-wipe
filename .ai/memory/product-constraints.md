# Beamo Wipe product constraints

Beamo Wipe is a guided UI on top of nwipe, sold as a pre-flashed USB. It is not a new wipe OS.

Amazon 1-stars on this aisle are: buyer thought plug-and-play then had to boot; stick never appeared; they wiped the USB; docs 404; reviewers noticed it is free ShredOS.

Keep nwipe underneath. Huge pick-the-disk + type-to-confirm. Name nwipe, post the GPL, no warranty. Do not claim DoD certificates or Apple Silicon.
