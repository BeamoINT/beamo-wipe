## Confirmed defects and fixes

1. **Mounted devices with unknown ancestry could leave their backing disk selectable.** Flat partition, crypt, LVM, RAID, multipath, or unknown dependent rows with absent/null/empty `PKNAME` were ignored even when mounted. Discovery now rejects that unresolved ancestry. Standalone optical, loop, and RAM roots retain their existing treatment; valid nested ancestry and a fresh complete inventory still work. Regressions cover malformed input, dependent types, recovery, and adjacent normal cases.
2. **Optional engine-log I/O errors prevented saving terminal evidence.** `read_export_log` handled open errors but let metadata, seek, and read errors escape. These now omit the optional log with status `unavailable`, retaining its authentication requirements and descriptor cleanup. Tests inject permission and I/O failures and exercise actual ordinary-directory bundle write/readback through a fake worker. The result JSON remains intact, and a subsequent explicit export includes the authenticated log again.
3. **Enter could request erasure while Back visibly held focus.** The Tk final screen deliberately focused Back, but its global Return handler selected erasure after the countdown. Return and keypad Enter now activate the focused enabled button on that screen. Absent/unrelated focus does not erase; explicit Erase focus still requires the countdown and a fresh keypress. The Tk hint and QEMU keyboard driver now require an explicit Tab to Erase. Console semantics are unchanged.

All reproductions used fake device metadata, intercepted/fake subprocess boundaries, dry-run wizards, and ordinary temporary files. No physical disk was opened or erased, and no VM was launched during the initial local stage.
