## Native probe observations

1. **Keyboard-inaccessible Show more:** Tk Label has takefocus `0` and only a
   Button-1 binding. Connection and path details behind it are mouse-only.
2. **Clipped device identifiers:** at 1024x740, a fake 108-character model needed
   1192 px and received 840 px; a fake 120-character serial needed 1485 px and
   received 840 px. Both lengths fit discovery's 128-character presentation cap.
   Card labels lack wrapping. The existing generic clipping helper excludes
   labels under canvases, where these cards live.
3. **Refresh blocks repainting:** a fake 400 ms rediscovery delayed a scheduled
   50 ms UI callback until _click_refresh returned, approximately 420 ms later.
   Tk calls refresh_disks synchronously and draws only after it completes.
4. **Enter ignores Back focus:** with the final screen ready and the Back button
   explicitly focused, _on_return invoked the intercepted erase callback. This
   matches the intentional global Enter mapping. It does not bypass the
   Wizard's authorization gates, but makes the visible focus misleading.

## Highest-value improvements

Prioritize focus-respecting destructive actions, keyboard-reachable details,
unclipped identity, asynchronous discovery feedback, adaptive layout/text scale,
and a clearly separated protected boot-USB card. Then improve method explanations,
progress phases, actionable offline help, and the second-USB report workflow.

Other review areas for the user-facing backlog: fewer introductory transitions;
power and lid guidance; early language/input/accessibility settings; native
control consistency; calmer warning hierarchy; grouped physical-device inventory;
stable selection and scrolling; clear refresh reset explanations; human-readable
reports; post-erase computer-reuse guidance; optional safe repeated sessions;
specific recovery instructions; stable early-startup failure screens; physical
hardware and novice usability acceptance; and closer preview/native parity.

Preserve boot exclusion, whole-disk identity, ownership, token, fresh preflight,
five-second delay, explicit start, nwipe-only erasure, truthful outcomes and
guarded report media. New media-format support or repeat-session workflows
would require separate safety design and regression coverage.
